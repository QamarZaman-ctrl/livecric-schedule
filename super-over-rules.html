What is a Super Over in Cricket? Rules, History & Over-Rate Laws

June 2026 By QZ Admin

Cricket dunya ka aik aisa khel hai jo hamesha se hi apnay unpredictable nature aur high-voltage drama ki wajah se jana jata hai[cite: 8]. Agar match aakhri ball tak chala jaye to fans ki dharkanein tez ho jati hain, lekin sochein kya ho agar dono teams ka score bilkul barabar ho jaye[cite: 8]? Yes, jab aik match block-buster tie par khatam hota hai, to dunya ka sab se bara cricket tiebreaker action mein aata hai, jisay hum Super Over kehte hain[cite: 8].

Jab koi limited-overs cricket match (jaise T20 ya ODI) khatam ho aur dono competing teams ke runs bilkul barabar (equal) hon, to match ka faisla karne ke liye aik extra over khela jata hai[cite: 8]. Is over mein dono teams ko 1, 1 over (yani 6 legal balls) khelne ka mauqa milta hai[cite: 8]. Jo team is single over mein sab se zyada runs banati hai, wo match ki winner declare ki jati hai[cite: 8].

History of the Super Over: From Bowl-Out to Modern Era

Tareekh par nazar dalein to ICC ne isay sab se pehle 2008 mein international cricket mein introduce kiya tha[cite: 8]. Is se pehle Twenty20 International matches mein tiebreaker ke liye aik bilkul alag tareeqa istemal hota tha jisay Bowl-Out kaha jata tha[cite: 8].

Bowl-Out rule bilkul football ke penalty shootout ki tarah hota tha, jahan dono teams ke 5, 5 bowlers bari bari aatay thay aur samnay khali stumps ko hit karne ki koshish karte thay (bina kisi batsman ke)[cite: 8]. Is ka sab se bara aur famous example 2007 ke T20 World Cup mein India vs Pakistan ka match تھا[cite: 8]. Lekin cricket experts ka manna tha ke yeh luck par zyadah depend karta hai, is liye ICC ne Bowl-Out ko hamesha ke liye khatam kar ke pure cricketing method introduce kiya, jisay aaj hum Super Over kehte hain[cite: 8].

How Does a Super Over Work? Step-by-Step

  1. The Nominations: Match tie hone ke foran baad, dono teams ke captains umpires ko apnay 3 main batsmen aur 1 main bowler ki list submit karte hain[cite: 8].
  2. The Ground Setup: Ground par sirf wahi selected fielding team, un ka bowler aur batting team ke 2 nominated batsmen aatay hain[cite: 8].
  3. The Batting Order: Jo team main match mein baad mein batting kar rahi thi (yani chasing team), wo Super Over mein pehle batting karne aati hai[cite: 8].
  4. The Target: Pehle batting karne wali team 6 balls par jitna bhi score banati hai, doosri team ko us se 1 run zyadah ka target milta hai[cite: 8].

Super Over ICC Rules: Batting & Bowling Laws

Super Over Batting Rules Cricket

  • The 3-Batsmen Limit: Kisi bhi Super Over mein aik team ke paas maximum 3 nominated batsmen hote hain[cite: 8]. Agar batting team ke 2 wickets gir jayen, to un ki innings wahin All Out ho jaye gi[cite: 8].
  • No Fresh Start for Stats: Super Over mein banaye gaye runs ya liye gaye wickets batsman ya bowler ke personal career statistics mein shamil nahi kiye jate[cite: 8].

Super Over Bowling Rules Cricket

  • Single Bowler Constraint: Kisi bhi aik Super Over mein sirf 1 nominated bowler hi poore 6 balls deliver kar sakta hai[cite: 8].
  • The Extra Ball Penalty: Agar bowler wide ball ya no-ball phenkta hai, to batting team ko penalty run milta hai aur bowler ko wo ball dobara phenkni parti hai[cite: 8]. No-ball par batsman ko Free Hit milti hai[cite: 8].

What Happens if a Super Over is Tied?

Purane rules ke mutabiq, agar Super Over tie ho jata to winner ka faisla Boundary Count Rule ke tehat hota tha, jahan total boundaries check ki jati thin (jaise 2019 World Cup final mein hua)[cite: 8]. Lekin is rule par dunya bhar mein itni controversy hui ke ICC ko apnay rules tabdeel karne paray[cite: 8].

Modern cricket aur Super Over cricket rules 2024 ke mutabiq, ab boundary count rule ko mukammal taur par ban kar diya gaya hai[cite: 8]. Naye rule ke mutabiq, agar aik Super Over tie ho jaye, to Consecutive Super Overs (yani doosra Super Over) khela jaye ga[cite: 8]. Yeh silsila tab tak chalta rahe ga jab tak koi aik team clear winner nahi ban jati[cite: 8]. Naye Super Over mein purana bowler bowling nahi kar sakta aur out huway batsmen dobara batting nahi kar sakte[cite: 8].

Famous Super Over Matches in Cricket History

  • India vs Afghanistan (2024 Bengaluru Test/T20): Yeh match modern cricket ka anokha shahkar tha jahan main game tie hui, pehla Super Over tie hua, aur doosray Super Over me India ne match jeeta[cite: 8].

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Super Over mein kitne players out ho sakte hain?
Super Over mein aik team ke maximum 2 players (wickets) out ho sakte hain, kyunke sirf 3 nominated batsmen submit kiye jate hain[cite: 8].
Q2: Kya fielding team Super Over mein review (DRS) le sakti hai?
Ji haan, limited overs cricket ke modern rules ke mutabiq teams ko Super Over mein bhi 1 DRS review per innings istemal karne ki ijazat hoti hai[cite: 8].
Q3: Super Over mein kon sa bowler ball phenk sakta hai?
Fielding team ka captain playing XI mein se kisi bhi 1 bowler ko chun sakta hai, chahe us bowler ne main match mein apnay overs poore kar liye hon ya na kiye hon[cite: 8].

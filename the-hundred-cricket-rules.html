The Ultimate Guide to The Hundred Cricket: Format, Rules, and Teams

June 2026 By QZ Admin

Cricket has evolved dramatically over the last few decades, adapting constantly to capture modern audiences[cite: 6]. From traditional five-day Test matches to action-packed three-hour T20s, the tactical dynamics keep shifting[cite: 6]. In 2021, the England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB) introduced a brand-new tournament format that turned heads worldwide: The Hundred[cite: 6].

At its core, the game is a simplified, accelerated version of limited-overs cricket[cite: 6]. Instead of counting traditional six-ball overs, the entire game revolves around a strict countdown of 100 legal deliveries per innings[cite: 6]. The team that scores the most runs in their 100 balls wins the match, leaving absolutely no room for defensive tactics[cite: 6].

The Hundred Cricket Rules & Scoring Format

The fundamental adjustments in the ECB format completely change how captains manage their bowlers and how batters approach their innings[cite: 6]:

  • 100 Balls Per Innings: Each team gets exactly 100 legal balls to build their total score[cite: 6].
  • The 10-Ball Change: Bowlers can deliver either 5 or 10 consecutive balls[cite: 6]. The captain decides whether a bowler stays on for a 10-ball block or switches after 5[cite: 6].
  • End Selection: The bowling ends change after every 10 balls, rather than after every 6 balls[cite: 6].
  • Maximum Balls Per Bowler: A single bowler can deliver a maximum of 20 legal balls per match[cite: 6]. Traditional "over" terminology is bypassed in official broadcasting[cite: 6].
  • Powerplay Restrictions: The first 25 balls constitute the Powerplay, where only two fielders are allowed outside the initial 30-yard fielding circle[cite: 6]. After 25 balls, up to four fielders can sit outside the boundary ring[cite: 6].
  • Strategic Timeout: Each bowling side is allowed a mandatory 2-minute strategic timeout mid-innings[cite: 6].
  • No-Balls and Wides: A No-Ball awards 2 penalty runs followed by a Free Hit[cite: 6]. Wides also award penalty runs, and the 100-ball count decreases only on completely legal deliveries[cite: 6].

The Hundred Cricket Teams List

Unlike standard English county cricket featuring 18 traditional counties, The Hundred consists of 8 newly created, city-based franchises[cite: 6]. Each franchise features both a men's and a women's team sharing the same brand identity and playing back-to-back on the same day[cite: 6]:

  1. Manchester Originals (Old Trafford, Manchester): Representing the North West of England with an aggressive batting identity[cite: 6].
  2. Oval Invincibles (The Kia Oval, London): Representing South London; historically one of the most successful setups in tournament history[cite: 6].
  3. London Spirit (Lord's, London): Based in North London at the historic "Home of Cricket," bringing massive international tactical minds[cite: 6].
  4. Southern Brave (Utilita Bowl, Southampton): Representing the southern coast with balanced express bowling matrices[cite: 6].
  5. Trent Rockets (Trent Bridge, Nottingham): Representing the East Midlands, capitalizing on short, high-scoring boundaries[cite: 6].
  6. Northern Superchargers (Headingley, Leeds): Representing Yorkshire and the North East with high fan-base energy[cite: 6].
  7. Birmingham Phoenix (Edgbaston, Birmingham): West Midlands hub famous for analytical short-format squad planning[cite: 6].
  8. Welsh Fire (Sophia Gardens, Cardiff): The single franchise based in Wales, driving immense national pride[cite: 6].

Player Draft & Elite Stars

To maintain balance, squads are locked using a standardized player draft based on pre-determined salary bands[cite: 6]. Each team can feature a specific quota of elite overseas players (typically up to 3 or 4 in the playing XI) alongside local domestic icons[cite: 6]. Top-tier short-format specialists like Rashid Khan, Jos Buttler, Ellyse Perry, and Liam Livingstone routinely dominate the leaderboards[cite: 6].

Pros, Cons, & Format Controversy

The tournament has carved a permanent niche but faces continuous debate among cricket purists[cite: 6]:

  • The Pros: It finishes within a strict 2.5-hour prime-time window[cite: 6]. Scheduling women's matches directly before men's matches has dramatically boosted global viewership and generated historic crowds for women's sports[cite: 6].
  • The Cons: Many longtime county loyalists strongly dislike the elimination of traditional overs[cite: 6]. The scheduling has forced traditional structures like the One-Day Cup and County Championship matches to the margins of the summer season[cite: 6].

Essential Fantasy Cricket Tips

Because of the rapid, 100-ball nature of the game, setting up fantasy teams requires unique, data-driven approaches[cite: 6]:

  • Prioritize All-Rounders: Multi-dimensional assets who bowl blocks of 10 balls and bat in the top 5 are high-yield options[cite: 6].
  • Focus on the Top 3: Lower-order hitters rarely get enough balls to construct massive scores; maximize your top-order allocation[cite: 6].
  • Target Death Bowlers: Bowlers delivering consecutive blocks at the tail-end (balls 80 to 100) have a much higher probability of picking up cheap wickets as batters slog blindly[cite: 6].

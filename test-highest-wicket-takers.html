In-Depth Legend Profiles: The Bowling Elite

1. Muttiah Muralitharan (800 Wickets)

When searching for who has the most wickets in test cricket, the crown belongs exclusively to Sri Lanka's spin wizard[cite: 10]. The Muralitharan record stands at an almost unreachable apex of 800 wickets[cite: 10]. His signature delivery, the Doosra, completely revolutionized the craft[cite: 10]. He holds the absolute records for the most 5-wicket hauls (67) and 10-wicket hauls (22) in history[cite: 10].

2. Shane Warne (708 Wickets)

Shane Warne was the absolute monarch of theater, drama, and tactical wizardry, accumulating 708 wickets[cite: 10].

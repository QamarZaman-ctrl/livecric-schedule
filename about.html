About Global Match Center

Welcome to Global Match Center, the premier digital hub for cricket enthusiasts worldwide. In a world where cricket is more than just a sport—it's a heartbeat—we strive to be the bridge between fans and the live action on the field.

Our Vision and Journey

Established in 2026, Global Match Center was born out of a simple necessity: providing a clean, fast, and highly accurate scheduling platform for cricket fans. We realized that while there are many sports sites, very few offer a dedicated, clutter-free experience specifically for match timings and fixtures across international and domestic circuits.

What We Cover

From the streets of Pakpattan to the massive stadiums of Melbourne and London, our reach is global. We provide detailed coverage of:

  • International Fixtures: ICC World Cups, Champions Trophy, and bilateral series.
  • Premier T20 Leagues: Deep dives into PSL (Pakistan Super League), IPL, Big Bash, and CPL.
  • Timings Precision: Specialized GMT and PKT conversion to ensure you never miss the first ball.

Meet the QZ Network

Global Match Center is a flagship project of the QZ Network. Led by a team of dedicated developers and cricket analysts, we combine technical expertise with a deep love for the game. Our platform is built on modern web technologies to ensure lightning-fast loading speeds, even on slow mobile networks.

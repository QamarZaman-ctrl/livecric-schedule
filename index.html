Detailed Technical Laws of Cricket

The Bat and Ball Specifications

The Ball: Must weigh between 155.9g to 163g. In Test matches, a red ball is used; in limited overs, a white ball.

The Bat: Width cannot exceed 4.25 inches, length not more than 38 inches.

Rare Dismissals

Obstructing the Field: Deliberate distraction of fielders.

Retired Out: Leaving field without permission and not returning.

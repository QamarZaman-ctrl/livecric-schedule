Last Updated: May 2026

At Global Match Center, accessible from livecricschedule.live, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Global Match Center and how we use it.

1. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

We are a Data Controller of your information. Global Match Center's legal basis for collecting and using the personal information described in this Privacy Policy depends on the context in which we collect the information:

  • Global Match Center needs to perform a contract with you.
  • You have given Global Match Center permission to do so.

2. Log Files

Global Match Center follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes IP addresses, browser type, ISP, date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

4. Our Advertising Partners

Some of advertisers on our site may use cookies and web beacons. Each of our advertising partners has their own Privacy Policy for their policies on user data.

5. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. Global Match Center does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

6. Contact Information

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@livecricschedule.live.
